About Us

A women-led movement bridging Africa’s digital divide.

TechShe Can Africa is a women-led social impact and technology initiative committed to empowering women in underserved communities across Africa through digital skills training, artificial intelligence, financial inclusion, and community-centered innovation.

What began with two donated laptops and a small community training session has grown into a movement focused on ensuring women are not left behind in Africa’s rapidly expanding digital economy.

Our Mission

To empower women in underserved communities across Africa with digital skills, AI-driven tools, and smart financial solutions.

Our Vision

To build a digitally empowered Africa where every woman has access to technology, opportunity, financial inclusion, and protection.

What We Do

Four programs. One ecosystem of empowerment.

Our programs combine digital training, safety, agriculture, finance, and AI to create lasting transformation for women and communities.

Digital Skills Training

Computer literacy, internet use, mobile money, social media marketing, and digital business tools.

HerVoice 360°

A secure platform supporting women experiencing gender-based violence through confidential reporting and access to support.

AgriAccess+

A digital agriculture and financial inclusion platform helping women farmers track income, expenses, and market data.

AI for Women in Business

Practical AI tools for customer service, analytics, inventory, forecasting, content, and design.

Founder’s Note

A Vision Rooted
in Experience

“When I started this initiative, I never imagined it would grow into a movement. I simply saw women around me who were working incredibly hard but lacked access to the tools that could help them grow.”

As the initiative grew, another challenge became impossible to ignore. Many women entrepreneurs were not only struggling economically, they were also facing fear, trauma, and insecurity caused by gender-based violence.

Economic empowerment without protection could never be enough. This reality inspired HerVoice 360°. At the same time, many women farmers lacked modern systems to manage farms and access financial services, which led to AgriAccess+.

“When women access technology, they don’t just change their own lives. They transform families. They strengthen communities. They shape the future.”

What began with two laptops has grown into a vision for a continental movement. And this is only the beginning.

Roselinda Keziah Wanam

Founder & CEO, TechShe Can Africa · CTO Partner, JeliData Tech Solutions
